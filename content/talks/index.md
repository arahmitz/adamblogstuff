## Czy musimy tyle klikać? (HelloGameDev Conference 2026) [PL]
![Czy musimy tyle klikać?](/images/talks/czymusimytyleklikac.jpg "[Presentaton available here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/e/2PACX-1vTdWizNTzwyU3SL-R1DLyOuORAPRddjJwDgZEbKxNqcgHb1XQq60OCtAJZAeDh3uaSY9vzRqMrP8aOi/pub?start=false&loop=false&delayms=3000)")

*Czy musimy tyle klikać?* is a talk I gave at HelloGameDev Conference 2026, focusing on writing your own automation scripts and custom tools for people working in DCC. It explores how repetitive tasks can be turned into scripts and simple tools that save time, and make workflows more efficient. 

The presentation follows practical examples from my own work, including tools I've written for my own team,  as well as a step-by-step example on of turning a manual process into a simple script using Script Editor & Python. I also touch on the UX of a good tool - from identifying repetitive processes and developing a useful solution, to choosing the right way to distrubute tools within a team. (Presentation in Polish)

## Animacyjne Dojo (Digital Night Studio/Lodz University of Technology 20024) [PL]
![Animacyjne Dojo Cover](/images/talks/animacyjnedojo_cover.jpg "[Presentation available here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/e/2PACX-1vSADucq-Mkmv9_flTBaHDkf3mogJBdUehcg0T6xx7BUkQURv_w4KIVLAtIQUPArO64mrpnpCvUndw5F/pub?start=false&loop=false&delayms=3000)")

*Animacyjne Dojo* is a presentation I gave for Digital Night Studio at Lodz University of Technology, focusing on gameplay animation. It provides an overview of the fundamental principles from a gameplay animator's perspective,
addresing daily tasks and unique challanges. The presentation covers essential animation exeercises such as *the bouncing ball* and *tails/squirrel*, offering tips and tricks for each. It concludes with resources and guidance for those
looking to learn animation and start their careers in the field. (*Presentation in Polish*)
