## Forever Skies (2025)
![Forever Skies main logo](/images/projects/foreverskiesmain.jpg "[Forever Skies (2025)](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1641960/Forever_Skies/_)/ [Far From Home](https://www.farfromhomegames.com/) - Animation, Rigging, Animation Implementation") 

**Forever Skies** is a co-op first-person post-apocalyptic survival game. Return to Earth, ravaged by an ecological disaster. Fly, upgrade and customize your mobile high-tech airship base. Scavenge resources to survive, face dangers on the surface and hunt for viral pathogens to cure a mysterious illness.

In the project, I was responsible for a broad range of the animation pipeline - from rigging and animation - to implementation in UE4. I adapted all existing FPP animations into TPP for the co-op integration, while also creating numerous animations including player actions, weapons, and cutscenes, as well as animations for structures and enemies. I was involved in implementing and supporting parts of the animation system in UE4, particularly those required to support FPP/TPP split for co-op. 

During post-launch support, I took the ownership of the whole animation system in-engine and continued supporting the projects rigging and animation needs. Alongside my core responsibilites, 
I regularly helped artists with asset integration and technical solutions to their ideas, often implemented these gameplay features myself. One example was the football minigame introduced in the Echoes Part 2 update, which I both designed and programmed.
